The strength of Blackman Detective Services lies in knowledge and experience.

Founded in Raleigh in 1993, Blackman Detective Services is one of North Carolina’s respected Private Investigation Firms, serving law firms, insurance professionals, businesses, and private citizens.

A firm built on experience.

Blackman Detective Services was founded by William Allison Blackman in 1993 after 30 years of exemplary service with the Raleigh Police Department, where he retired as a homicide investigator.

The firm offers a full line of investigative services, including a well-established practice in workers’ compensation insurance claims. Clients range from large international corporations to private citizens, and the team is dedicated to understanding the complex issues each client encounters.

Blackman Detective Services is licensed by the North Carolina Private Protective Services Board and fully complies with North Carolina State Law.

Professional foundation

Licensed North Carolina investigators with reciprocal agreements and affiliations that allow the firm to operate in many places beyond the state.

Leadership and Legacy

The firm’s leadership combines police-level investigative experience, mentorship, community involvement, and a continued commitment to client service.

Owner and Licensed Private Investigator

Jamie Mitchell

Blackman Detective Services proudly re-introduced itself as minority- and female-owned in 2019 when it was purchased by licensed Private Investigator and bondsman Jamie Mitchell.

Jamie spent five years working full-time with Blackman Detective Services under the mentorship of Mr. Blackman and Mr. Bissette. Her ambition, attention to detail, and community involvement have helped shape her career as a respected investigator.

Founder

William Allison Blackman

Renowned Private Investigator William Allison Blackman founded Blackman Detective Services after retiring from the Raleigh Police Department, where he served for 30 years.

Mr. Blackman’s background as a homicide investigator and his dedication to client service helped establish a firm known for work with law firms, insurance professionals, government agencies, businesses, and individuals.
