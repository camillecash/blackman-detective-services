Legal & Court Support

Court Testimony

When necessary, our investigators are available to provide professional testimony regarding their documented findings.

Criminal Defense Investigations

Every case deserves a thorough investigation. From misdemeanors to complex felony matters, our experienced investigators work alongside defense attorneys to uncover facts, locate and interview witnesses, review evidence, and identify information critical to the defense.

Statements & Interviews

Our investigators can conduct professional interviews and document statements through written, audio, or video formats for legal and investigative matters.

Service of Process

Finding them is one service. Serving them is another. Blackman Detective Services provides professional service of legal documents throughout North Carolina. Our investigators approach each assignment with discretion, persistence, and detailed documentation—particularly when dealing with individuals who may be difficult to locate or intentionally avoiding service.

Litigation & Trial Support

Our investigators provide comprehensive support to attorneys throughout the litigation process, including due diligence, witness research, jury panel research, background investigations, and other case-specific investigative needs.

02

Insurance & Claims

Disability Claims Investigations

We will work for you to provide information on the present activities, recovery, and progress of a claimant’s condition.

Insurance Case Surveillance

Effective surveillance requires experience, strategy, and attention to detail. Blackman Detective Services provides professional investigative support for a variety of insurance-related matters. Our investigators conduct discreet, objective surveillance designed to document activity, identify relevant patterns, and provide clear, detailed findings while remaining mindful of the client’s investigative objectives and budget.

Personal Injury Investigations

The details matter. Our investigators support personal injury cases by locating witnesses, verifying information, conducting research, and documenting relevant activity to uncover facts that may be important to your case.

Workers’ Compensation Investigations

Facts matter when evaluating a claim. Blackman Detective Services provides professional investigative support for workers’ compensation matters, including surveillance, background research, activity verification, witness interviews, and documentation of relevant findings. Our investigators deliver clear, objective reports and are available to provide testimony when necessary.

Liability & Accident Investigations

When the facts surrounding an incident are in question, a thorough investigation can make the difference. Blackman Detective Services conducts independent investigations involving accidents, injuries, and potential liability. Our investigators work to identify witnesses, verify circumstances, document relevant information, and develop the facts surrounding an incident.

Investigations may involve motor vehicle accidents, premises liability, workplace incidents, product-related claims, personal injury matters, and other circumstances where liability may be disputed.

03

People & Family Matters

Background Checks

Let us provide comprehensive background checks, including civil and criminal checks. We do checks in North Carolina and nationwide that include DMV record searches. We can also obtain a resume, education, and employment verification, as well as credit histories. We even do social security number verifications and death index searches. Our firm can also obtain marriage records, credit reports (with signed authorization), and corporation partnership affiliations.

Child Custody & Special Investigations

When the well-being of your family is at stake, facts matter. Our investigators provide thorough, objective documentation for child custody matters, including surveillance, activity verification, and other case-specific investigative needs. We also conduct specialized investigations tailored to the unique circumstances and objectives of each client.

Domestic Investigations

Sensitive situations require experienced investigators. Our team understands the complexities of domestic investigations and approaches every case with discretion, professionalism, and care. We listen, assess your investigative needs, and use our experience to help guide you in the right direction—providing the facts and documentation you need to make informed decisions.

Locating Persons

Finding someone starts with knowing where to look. Our experienced research team uses professional investigative resources and proven techniques to locate individuals for a variety of personal and professional matters. Whether you’re searching for a witness, debtor, family member, former associate, or another hard-to-find individual, we provide accurate, discreet research designed to give you the answers you need.

Telephone Number Research

Unfamiliar number? Let us help identify who may be behind it. Blackman Detective Services provides professional research to assist in identifying unknown telephone numbers connected to your investigation. Whether the number appears in records relevant to a domestic, legal, or other investigative matter, our research team works discreetly to uncover available identifying information and provide you with useful, factual results.
